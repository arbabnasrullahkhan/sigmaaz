Frequently Asked Questions

How long do withdrawal requests take?
Withdrawals to EasyPaisa, JazzCash, and local banks are normally verified and dispatched within a few hours. Bank IBFT clearing may vary slightly during holiday clearing windows.
How does the 24-hour profit cycle operate?
Each active portfolio operates on its own dedicated 24-hour cycle calculated from the exact minute your deposit proof was approved. Yields are credited directly to your Withdrawable Profit Wallet.
What if my Transaction ID was rejected?
Verify that your payment slip matches the beneficiary account details and contains a unique, unsubmitted reference number. If an error persists, open a support ticket below with the exact TID.
